# Demo and release story

This is the meeting-friendly story for explaining HealthForge quickly and consistently.

## One-sentence pitch

HealthForge turns public healthcare regulations and standards references into grounded, reviewable engineering planning artifacts.

## One-minute version

HealthForge is a governed AI workflow platform for healthcare interoperability teams. It ingests approved public sources, retrieves cited evidence, helps users create reviewable Briefs, captures human approvals and audit history, and then supports implementation-oriented handoff through governed exports, workspace workflows, and trust dashboards.

## What to highlight in a demo

### 1. This is not just search

Search finds passages.

HealthForge adds:

- review workflow
- explicit approval
- auditability
- reusable workspace structure
- governed downstream handoff

### 2. This is not just chat

Chat can generate an answer.

HealthForge keeps:

- evidence visible
- findings inspectable
- workflow roles explicit
- boundaries honest

### 3. This is not trying to replace human reviewers

The point is to make the path from evidence to approved planning cleaner and more traceable.

## Suggested talk tracks

### For product leaders

“We are building a governed interoperability research and planning layer, not a generic assistant.”

### For architects

“We can show source-backed planning logic, workflow mapping, and FHIR-oriented guidance without burying the evidence.”

### For enterprise or compliance stakeholders

“The system is bounded by design, makes approval explicit, and keeps trust surfaces visible.”

### For builders

“The platform now gives you reviewed planning outputs, workspace context, and implementation starting points through the UI, API, CLI, SDK, and IDE companion.”

## Recommended demo order

1. Open the reviewer workspace.
2. Ask a prior-authorization question.
3. Inspect cited findings.
4. Create or inspect a Brief.
5. Show review and approval posture.
6. Switch to auditor or administrator.
7. Show trust, posture, and readiness views.
8. Close with the architecture diagram and product boundary.

## Good closing line

“HealthForge is strongest today where teams need grounded evidence, governed review, and a credible path from interpretation to implementation planning.”
