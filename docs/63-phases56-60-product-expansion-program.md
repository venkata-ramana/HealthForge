# Phases 56–60 — Product expansion program

Phases 56–60 extend HealthForge beyond platform readiness into repeatable customer value. The aggregate operator surface is:

```text
GET /v1/enterprise/product-expansion
```

It is restricted to auditors and administrators and returns delivered capability, evidence, remaining actions, gates, and limitations.

| Phase | Focus | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 56 | Governed live integrations | Governed execution ready |
| 57 | Evidence intelligence and answer quality | Quality operations ready |
| 58 | Collaborative research and workflow automation | Workspace workflow ready |
| 59 | FHIR implementation artifact exchange | Artifact handoff ready |
| 60 | Customer outcomes and product intelligence | Outcome measurement ready |

## Product gates

Before expanding these capabilities, the owner should verify connector approval and rollback, evidence-quality targets, team ownership and escalation, artifact versioning and validation, and privacy-reviewed customer outcome metrics.

## Scope boundary

This phase set completes the product-expansion foundation. It does not guarantee external connector success, clinical outcomes, compliance, or PHI handling. Live integrations, registries, notification providers, and telemetry policies remain deployment-specific.
