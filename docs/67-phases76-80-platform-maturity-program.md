## Maturity gates

Before broad ecosystem growth, verify API compatibility, model-change safety review, auditable privacy decisions, partner certification ownership, and evidence-based portfolio decisions.

## Scope boundary

This phase set completes a platform-maturity foundation. It does not provide compliance certification, clinical safety certification, market success, or commercial guarantees. Developer portals, policy automation, privacy systems, partner programs, and investment decisions require named owners.
