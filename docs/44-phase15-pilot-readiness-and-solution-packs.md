# Phase 15: pilot readiness and solution packs

Phase 15 turns HealthForge from a strong showcase product into a stronger private-pilot conversation.

The goal is not to pretend the platform is already a regulated production system. The goal is to make pilot readiness, audience fit, reporting, and future-state governance easier to discuss honestly and repeatedly.

## What shipped

- pilot-readiness checklist and bounded readiness artifacts
- solution packs for provider, payer, and platform/interoperability audiences
- stakeholder reporting pack built from live product state
- future control roadmap for identity, governance, and regulated deployment conversations
- pilot success-plan workflows with milestones, owners, and adoption signals

## Why it matters

Before this phase, HealthForge could already demonstrate:

- grounded evidence retrieval
- reviewable Brief workflows
- approval and auditability
- enterprise operations and governance surfaces

Phase 15 adds the missing commercial and pilot-execution layer around those capabilities:

- buyers can understand whether the product is ready for a bounded private evaluation
- demos can be tailored more cleanly by user segment
- leadership and evaluators can receive a repeatable reporting artifact
- enterprise stakeholders can discuss the future control path without confusion
- pilot execution can be structured around milestones instead of loose follow-up notes

## New product surfaces

### Pilot readiness

Shows:

- readiness tier
- bounded pilot checks
- pilot evaluation artifacts
- explicit current limits

Use it to explain:

- whether HealthForge is ready for a non-production private pilot
- what “ready” means in practice
- why readiness is different from certification or compliance claims

### Solution packs

Shows:

- provider pack
- payer pack
- platform/interoperability pack

Use it to explain:

- which workflows matter most for each audience
- how to tailor a demo without improvising from scratch
- how trust and delivery narratives stay aligned with shipped capabilities

### Stakeholder reporting pack

Shows:

- executive summary
- delivery summary
- trust summary
- reporting artifacts by audience

Use it to explain:

- what leadership should know now
- what evaluators should inspect next
- what operators and pilot owners should follow up on

### Future control roadmap

Shows:

- current versus target state
- roadmap tracks for identity, compliance-control mapping, and regulated deployment
- guardrails for discussing the roadmap honestly

Use it to explain:

- where the platform is today
- where it could go next
- how to avoid overstating current maturity

### Pilot success plan

Shows:

- adoption signals from the live workspace
- milestone tracking
- named owner roles
- follow-up patterns

Use it to explain:

- how a pilot will be judged
- who owns each milestone
- whether the pilot is progressing in a structured way

## Demo path

1. Switch to `auditor`
2. Open `Pilot readiness`
3. Open `Stakeholder report`
4. Switch to `reviewer`
5. Open `Solution packs`
6. Open `Pilot success`
7. Switch back to `auditor`
8. Open `Future roadmap`

That gives a clean Phase 15 story:

- are we ready for a pilot?
- for whom?
- how do we report progress?
- what comes next after a successful private evaluation?

## Boundaries

Phase 15 still keeps the same product boundaries in place:

- public, non-sensitive sources only
- synthetic or non-sensitive workflow and FHIR examples only
- human review required
- no PHI handling
- no claim of compliance certification
- no claim of production-readiness

The difference is that HealthForge can now support a much more credible private-pilot discussion while staying honest about those boundaries.
