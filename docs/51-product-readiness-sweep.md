# Product readiness sweep

Date: Wednesday, July 29, 2026

## Executive summary

HealthForge is ready for credible demos, internal planning workflows, synthetic interoperability exercises, and enterprise-style architecture conversations.

The highest-value next build track is to strengthen evidence operations and answer quality so the product becomes more dependable for repeated analyst use, not just guided showcase use.

## What is strong right now

### Product story

- clear problem framing
- strong architecture narrative
- bounded and honest product posture
- multiple audience-specific demo paths

### Workflow depth

- grounded retrieval and cited findings
- reviewable Brief workflow with approvals and audit history
- prior-authorization and FHIR planning surfaces
- team workspace and governed handoff patterns

### Trust and governance

- evaluation and policy/safety reporting
- enterprise posture and regulated-readiness views
- explicit role-based flows for reviewer, approver, auditor, and administrator

## What still limits product readiness

### Evidence freshness and coverage

The product is strongest when the question fits the current curated corpus. It is weaker when users ask broader or newer questions and need help understanding whether the issue is:

- missing source coverage
- weak query phrasing
- insufficient evidence
- outdated source selection

### Repeated analyst workflow

The platform has good demo and governance depth, but repeated day-to-day research workflows still need stronger support for:

- saved question packs
- precedent comparison
- research continuity
- evidence-diff and source-change visibility

### Operationalized delivery loops

Governed delivery scaffolding exists, but the strongest near-term value is still upstream in evidence, review, and planning rather than downstream real-system execution.

## Highest-value next build track

### Recommendation

Prioritize an Evidence Operations and Research Quality track.

### Why this is the best next move

It improves the core product loop directly:

1. users ask a better question
2. HealthForge finds better evidence
3. users understand when evidence is insufficient
4. approved outputs become more trustworthy and repeatable

That creates more product value than widening the surface area again before the evidence loop is more dependable.

## Recommended Phase 21–25 arc

### Phase 21: evidence operations and retrieval quality

- source freshness tracking
- evidence sufficiency diagnostics
- query-refinement guidance
- citation and snapshot-diff visibility

### Phase 22: analyst research workspace

- saved question packs
- precedent Brief comparison
- reusable evidence collections
- research continuity workflows

### Phase 23: governed delivery operationalization

- stronger live-capable connector controls
- delivery reconciliation and status tracking
- inbound task-to-Brief traceability
- operator-grade connector audit views

### Phase 24: domain solution packaging

- reusable domain packs for prior auth and adjacent interoperability workflows
- persona-specific demo paths
- workflow presets and guided configuration

### Phase 25: pilot adoption and product instrumentation

- usage and funnel analytics
- workflow outcome tracking
- stakeholder-ready reporting
- pilot success and adoption dashboards

## Bottom line

HealthForge has already earned the right to be shown to people.

The next big step is not “more features everywhere.” It is making the evidence-to-Brief loop stronger, clearer, and more reusable so the product feels dependable even outside a guided demo.
